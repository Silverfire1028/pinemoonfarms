After taking a year off of canning (and nearly going crazy in the process) I have canned my first jars of jelly. What kind, you ask? Dandelion.

I made dandelion jelly.

I have been wanting to try canning and preserving things found in nature. Good practice, just in case you know? So I tried it out.

It smells like honey. Looks like it too. Can't say I've tried it yet, all of it went in the jars. But I can't wait to make biscuits.

Here's the link to the recipe I used if you feel like foraging yourself.  http://www.simplycanning.com/dandelion-jelly.html

In other news, it's been raining every day for the past week. We finally got to see some sun today. The blue sky and yellow sunshine really lifted the mood on the farm. The cows are busy gestating and we should be seeing some new faces in the next couple months.

Chickens are in the near future and I am so excited for eggs again! I miss my home-grown eggs.

Dad's busy recovering from his surgery. He's doing much better than last time, as he has feeling in his fingertips this time.

Not too exciting this week. Just day-to-day life on the farm.
