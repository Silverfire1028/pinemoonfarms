## [On my Hiatus…](https://hotmesstohomestead.wordpress.com/2015/02/14/on-my-hiatus/)

So, it's been a while. A long while. We have been busy over the past months, building our home and taking care of the animals. Everyone is surviving the frigid temperatures here in mid-Ohio, even if we have to wear four layers just to step out our door!
Our cattle are all fat and happy, as well as our horses. Actually, a couple are a little too fat and happy!
Sapphire, my Rottie/Lab, hates the cold and refuses to stay outside or more than five minutes, much to the dismay of Chico, our very long haired mutt. His favorite pass time is rolling in the snow, and tracking it through the house.
I'm going to try to keep up with this blog, as we are making progress on the homestead and want to keep everyone updated!
Stay tuned for more from Knotty Knuff Acres! Hope you are warm, wherever you are!
