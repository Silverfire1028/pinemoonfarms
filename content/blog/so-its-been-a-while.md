## [So, It's Been a While…](https://hotmesstohomestead.wordpress.com/2024/07/23/so-its-been-a-while/)

I am sitting in my living room right now as I type this. Older, sicker, but still determined to make a living off of my farm. I have an unreasonable amount of chicks on order (chicken math got me!) for the 5th of August. The plan here is to sell some chicks as they grow, to offset the amount I spent, and also to reduce their numbers a bit!

Truth be told, I am ready for babies. It has been a long while since the farm has had younglings on it; so long in fact, that I was seriously considering getting a puppy! We as a household are not ready for a puppy, but it was a close thing. What we are ready for is some animals that “work for their keep” again. My current flock of Buckeye, Welsummer, and Easter Eggers are preforming poorly, and have been all year. I'm not sure what to do with them at this point, and I have their replacements on order. The horses just turn grass into fertilizer, and are not at work currently. There are plans for a horse drawn sled to retrieve maple sap from our maple grove, but are on the back burner at the moment.

Chickens are taking primary focus over the next couple years and, hopefully, beyond.

The summer is rapidly drawing to a close, and my health has prevented me from doing what I want, but I am committed to making the next year different.
