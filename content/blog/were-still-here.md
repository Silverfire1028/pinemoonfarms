## [We're still here!](https://hotmesstohomestead.wordpress.com/2016/05/04/were-still-here/)

I know, I know… it's been a long time since I wrote. We have been having a lot of trouble on the farm, and honestly, writing a blog and posting updates took a backseat to life.

Read on and I will explain. If you don't want to read all of what follows, skip to the end for farm updates! It begins at the *.

Firstly, I want to update you on my father's condition. He is going in for his second hand surgery in the next few days. He is barely able to use his right hand, and we are hoping that this surgery will allow him at least partial function.  He's holding strong, but is quite annoyed that he can't swing a hammer or pull a rope.

Secondly, and the main reason why I haven't updated anyone, is the fact we almost lost our farm at the tail end of last year. We were turned in to the local zoning commission, and they decided to go against what we were told 5 years ago. When we bought the place, they said that the existing building could be turned into a house without an issue. As we didn't get it in writing, we don't have any proof of them telling us this. They decided they didn't want a working farm in the middle of suburbia  (even though the whole township is zoned agricultural), so they recanted that assurance, and said it was too close to the road and we had to quit building. A lot of paperwork, finding loopholes, and $500 we didn't have later, we are approved for a variance,  conditionally. Sigh…

And lastly, I have been struggling with medical conditions myself for the last 6 months or so. I am crippled by severe and constant nausea. I know that doesn't sound all that bad, but I'll explain. For the longest time, I couldn't get anything to stay down. Food, water, stomach acid, it didn't matter. I couldn't eat for days on end, and could barely get out of bed, let alone work around the farm. I have to say, I got pretty low for a while. But. We pulled through. I am now on medication that allows me to function at least simi-normally while they search for a cause. I have a doctor willing to do anything to help. So, all in all, it's getting better.

*Still reading? Awesome. Now that the pity party explanation is over, I have an update! We are getting ready to put our garden in, which will be my first no-till garden. Yay for experimentation!

Our plants are growing fast. They are only about an inch high, but soon we will have heirloom veggies to plant.

We are going to get chickens, and will be taking orders in the next few months. I will update when we begin.

I am going to try to update every week on Friday at the latest. Forgive me if I skip a week. Life is rough but I am trying my best.
