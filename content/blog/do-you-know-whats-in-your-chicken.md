## [Do you know what's in your chicken?](https://hotmesstohomestead.wordpress.com/2020/10/05/do-you-know-whats-in-your-chicken/)

Do you know what's in your chicken and how it was raised? Do you know how to spot buzzwords on food packaging, or are you paying more because companies rely on you to fall for those buzzwords? Read on to learn how chicken companies use these words to trick you into paying more for industry standard, and why you should choose Just Our Chicken.

Per, USDA law, no chicken in the grocery store can be fed growth hormones (more info here https://tinyurl.com/y6beumba) and antibiotic use is sparse. Ionophores and non-antibiotic coccidiostats are now fed as a precaution to keep disease at bay in the huge flocks at factory farms (https://www.nationalchickencouncil.org/questions-answers-antibiotics-chicken-production/). “Cage Free” and “free range” can mean mass produced, floor raised birds. Take a look at this popular chicken supplier's website (https://www.perdue.com/perdue-way/organic/). They imply their birds are “free ranged”, but they only eat 100% vegetarian grain? Ignoring the fact that chickens are naturally omnivores (eat both meat and plants) how are you controlling their diet if they are “free ranging”? Truth is, these words are just buzzwords and companies rely on them to make their product look good. And while “cage free” sounds nice, when that many birds are housed together, ammonia levels can become dangerous. It doesn't take much to have harmful effects (https://tinyurl.com/y344cgbm).
