

## [Not Much of an Update](https://hotmesstohomestead.wordpress.com/2014/10/15/not-much-of-an-update/)

Over the past week, we have been busy getting ready for winter. The planning and prep that you must do on a farm is astounding. Right now, we are in the planning stage. We have decided how much wood we need to get us through the cold months, how much hay/feed we need, what weatherization we need to do to keep heat from leaking out of this house like water through a sieve, etc.

Nothing you can do fully prepares you for winter in the country. You just do your best, and hope you don't have to dig yourself out of a 6′ drift (this is not an exaggeration. We routinely dig out of 3-4′ drifts around here.) The wind cuts like a knife when it comes over the fields with no windbreak.

We live in a farm house that is closing in on it's centennial birthday, and when the wind blows, my curtains billow. With the windows closed. I have seen snow blow through the cracks around my windows. Needless to say, it gets cold here. I have woken up with an ice crust in a water bottle that was beside my bed. Thankfully, this doesn't happen often, but it has happened. My favorite (or least favorite, depending on how you look at it) memory is last year, I decided to boil a large pot of water to add heat and humidity to the air in my house. It was already so cold in my house that my stove couldn't heat it up to boiling point. It lost heat as fast as it gained it. That was a rough week. We ended up sleeping in the kitchen.

Hopefully, that won't happen this year. Regardless, we are preparing for the worst and hoping for the best. We started cutting more firewood today, and have much more to cut.

Over the weekend, we did get the overhang put on the back of the house, and finished putting up the siding. So now we have an entire side of siding done, plus the back windows put in, and the back door installed. We are getting ready to finish putting up the ply-wood on the garage side of the house. We are hoping to get the front windows framed in and the siding put up on the front of the house this week.

That's about it. Sorry I haven't updated in almost a week. I will try to keep this updated more frequently. See you all next time!
