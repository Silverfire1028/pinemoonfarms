## [Ode To Fridge](https://hotmesstohomestead.wordpress.com/2024/07/29/ode-to-fridge/)

> Oh, ‘fridge, so shiny and square,
> To thine coldness, nothing could compare.
> Protector of cheeses, both soft and hard;
> Milk, mayo, that cake from Barb.
> Chicken and beef,
> Those eggs, half a quiche
> All safe inside your shelf lined doors.
> And cleaning you, Ah! What a unwelcome chore!
> But one day, no coolness you inside kept,
> The air hot, the ice wept.
> So an end in flame, your life drew to a close
> Unceremoniously thrown to the scrap heap;
> Off you goes!
> A new shiny ‘fridge stands where you once stood.
> Go rest my savior of leftovers, you did good.
> – Silver

So two days ago (a Friday, of course) the defroster went out in the freezer side of our refrigerator. In the attempt to breathe new life into the fridge, we started melting the ice off the condenser coils. Unfortunately, we did too good of a job, and melted the interior to the point it wouldn't run. It was late, and the best we could do was pack what we wanted to save in coolers and ice, put two bags of ice in the top of the refrigerator (like an old fashioned ice chest would be) and hope for the best in this heat.

To my surprise, everything made it through the night. Thankfully, our neighbors let us use freezer space, so nothing was lost there either. Our new (to us) refrigerator was delivered Saturday morning, and is working like a champ.

Just a word to the wise, don't use a bullet heater to thaw out a fridge.

Remember, we all make mistakes, some small, some large. Sometimes we melt our fridge. But the important thing is to learn while making mistakes so you can make less of them in the future and never melt a fridge again.
