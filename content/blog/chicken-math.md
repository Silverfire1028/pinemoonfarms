title: Chicken Math
date: 2024-07-24
---

Ever been a victim of Chicken Math? What is Chicken Math, you ask? Chicken Math is acquiring more chickens than you have space for or ever intended to own, mostly through no fault of your own (haha). Chicken Math is like this… I want a rooster, but the recommended hen to rooster ratio is 6 at least… So I have to get 6… I also want this different breed, so I'll get one of those, too. And a rooster so I can breed. I can't just get 2! What if one dies! Best to get 3. Hmmm…I get a discount on 6 or more chicks, so I might as well get 6… It's not that much more. Oh! Look! This chicken is on sale, and I've always wanted a (insert breed here)! What is a couple more chickens? And on and on, until you have accidently bought 112 chickens, including 28 roosters and several expensive breeds.

Ask me how I know,

I am a victim of Chicken Math.

My chicken math is about to be reckoned with. My chicks are in the brooder at the hatchery, right now. They are two weeks old and (hopefully) growing well, and I couldn't be more stoked to finally get some babies to take care of. But like, 112 chickens? I'm freaking crazy. I do this to myself every time, and I have yet to learn. I complain and moan while I am on chick duty 24/7 for a few weeks, but I secretly love it. I adore watching the babies grow and learn, see their little personalities developed, and watch them transform seemingly on a daily basis. It is an amazing process, and I love every minute.

But, 112 chickens? Seriously?

Now, let me be clear. I have room and the supplies and ability to take care of 112 chickens for a short time. The plan is to sell some, and keep 4-6 of each breed, plus a rooster. The goal is to end up with 70ish chickens, including the roosters. There will be a bachelor pen for the boys, and the girls will free range until they need to go into the breeding pens in the spring. All will be taken care of, until they are sold or reach butcher age. But I haven't had this many chickens at once in YEARS. l think the last time I did 100 chickens, they were Cornish Cross and that was in the late 2000's. I was young and spry. Not so much now! At least these will be cleaner.

For those curious, the breeds I have purchased are: Black Copper Marans, Blue Copper Marans, Buckeye, Crested Cream Legbar, Cochin (Blue, White, and Partridge Color), Cuckoo Marans, Exchequer Leghorn, Olive Egger, Splash/Blue Ameracauna, and Welsummer. They were bought at Meyer Hatchery (https://meyerhatchery.com/). They are the best hatchery for chicks that I have found. All these chicks breeds will be for sale, started and ready to go on or around August 26th, 2024.
