## [Potential](https://hotmesstohomestead.wordpress.com/2015/03/23/potential/)

I'm sitting here, looking out a window that last year didn't exist. Nothing I could have done could have prepared me for the journey I have taken with my father over the past year. I thought I had an idea, but boy, was I wrong.

See, a year ago, the wall that holds this window wasn't here. Well, it was, but it was just a bunch of wooden slats to hold corn. The floor holding my chair wasn't here. This building was just a barn. Just a barn, I thought. But my father could see something I couldn't.

Potential.

And so we began. Clearing out the barn of all the memories of years. The scrap, the old motors, tires, an old swing. We came across someone's art supplies; old broken chalk and paintbrushes. Paint long dried up. And a drawing done in chalk of people, all colors and walks of life, on the back of some scrap wood. It wasn't finished. I saw something there, so I kept it.

Potential.

We placed beams for the floor,and plywood on the beams. We nailed and leveled; tore down and restarted. We designed and redesigned. We planed and worked. And people couldn't see what I could now see. What my father saw before we even began.

Potential.

Now, a year later, everyone can see what we saw. This old barn, built over 60 years ago, to house corn and tractors, now will house us. A building that no one gave a second thought to; a building that no one saw any use for, except to hold trash, will now shelter and house a family. All because someone saw something there that no one else could see.

Potential.
