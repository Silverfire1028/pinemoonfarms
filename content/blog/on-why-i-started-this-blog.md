## [On why I started this blog…](https://hotmesstohomestead.wordpress.com/2014/10/08/on-why-i-started-this-blog/)

So this is the question that I wondered all last night and through today. What is the reason for me to start this blog now, so late in the game? I mean, it has been 2 years since we started this little project. I had plenty of time to start one, but didn't. Why now? I asked myself.

It's for a few reasons. Firstly, I feel that this is a project that not many get to do, building their own homes from the ground up. Many people I talk to just don't understand how the walls around them came into being, or how much work it is just to put in an item as simple as a doorway. They don't understand the reason their house/apartment is laid out he way it is, or how the plumbing and electrical work is done. Or the importance of air flow, traffic flow, light flow, and insulation. I didn't, when I first started. Now I grasp just a bit better as to why things are the way they are.

Secondly, I need a place where I can put my thoughts, fears, desires, problems, etc. down on paper (errr…. Screen?). I don't expect people to read and wait with bated breath for my next post, but if I can organize my thoughts, and maybe teach someone along the way, I will be happy.

Lastly, and here is the point I just thought of not an hour ago, and the reason for this post, this blog is (hopefully) a way to keep me accountable and on track. I tend to procrastinate with things like housework and my writing. I hope that by keeping a daily journal on this blog, where maybe someone might see it and call me out on my laziness, will keep me on track.

So that's it, a few reasons for this blog and why I have it. I guess I just needed to justify me doing something I said made no sense and that I would never do. Strange how that works out, isn't it?
